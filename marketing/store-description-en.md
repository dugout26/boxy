## Long Description (Play 4000 / App Store 4000 chars)

```
🧩 Boxy - Pack & Fit Puzzle

Pack books, water bottles, and snacks into a school bag — without leaving any gaps. Rotate, fit, and feel that satisfying click.

Almost fits... but one cell short? Try rotating it. Just one more try.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

✨ Features

• 50 levels across 3 themes — School Bag → Moving Box → Travel Trunk
• Intuitive drag + long-press rotation (0.5s)
• 1-3 star system — clear without hints to earn ★★★
• Cozy, friendly design with our mascot Boxy
• Optional rewarded ads for hints and undos
• Remove ads with a one-time purchase ($2.99)

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🎮 How to play

1. Look at the empty cells in the bag
2. Drag items from the tray below
3. Doesn't fit? Long-press to rotate 90°
4. Fill every cell to clear the level

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

💛 Who's this for?

• Commute or break-time brain teasers
• Fans of cozy puzzle games over flashy graphics
• Lovers of jigsaw, tangram, block puzzles
• Anyone looking for a calm, no-pressure game
• Players who want a clean experience (ads-free option available)

🌟 The Mound series

Boxy is the first game in Mound's cozy puzzle series.
Same mascot, more games coming — Boxy Sort (June 2026), Boxy Cafe (July 2026).
```
