## 1. Information Collection Purpose

Mound (the "Company") operates the mobile game "Boxy" (the "Service") and processes personal information for:

1. **Service Provision**: Game progress save, level unlock management
2. **Advertising**: Rewarded / interstitial ads (AppLovin MAX, Google AdMob)
3. **Payments**: In-app purchases (Google Play Billing, Apple App Store)
4. **Analytics**: User behavior analysis for service improvement (Firebase Analytics)
5. **Attribution**: Install source tracking (AppsFlyer)
6. **Crash Diagnostics**: Crash report collection (Firebase Crashlytics)
7. **Customer Support**: Email inquiry responses

---

## 2. Information Collected

The Company collects the following:

### Required (essential to use service)
- **Device identifier** (advertising ID — IDFA on iOS, GAID on Android)
- **Game progress data** (current level, stars earned, items used) — stored locally on device only
- **Crash logs** (when crashes occur)

### Optional (with your consent)
- **Personalized ads** (when you explicitly consent via ATT/UMP popup)

### Not collected
- Real name, address, phone number, social security number
- Photos, contacts, location, microphone, camera
- Payment information (handled directly by Apple App Store / Google Play)

---

## 3. Retention Period

- **Game progress**: Stored locally on device. Deleted when you uninstall.
- **Advertising ID**: Per AppLovin / AdMob retention (typically 90 days)
- **Analytics data**: Per Firebase retention (60 days for events, 14 months for user-level)
- **Crash logs**: Per Firebase Crashlytics (90 days)
- **Email support**: Stored only for inquiry-response cycle, then deleted

---

## 4. Third-Party Sharing

The Company does NOT sell personal information. Information is shared only with:

| Party | Purpose | Data Shared |
|---|---|---|
| AppLovin MAX (Ad mediator) | Ad serving | Advertising ID, app version |
| Google AdMob (Ad network) | Ad serving | Advertising ID, country |
| Apple Sign-In / Google Play Billing | Payment | Transaction ID (no payment data) |
| Firebase (Analytics + Crashlytics) | Analysis + diagnostics | Anonymous events, crash stack |
| AppsFlyer | Attribution | Advertising ID |
| Apple Inc. / Google LLC | Platform-required | App Store / Play Store standard data |

---

## 5. Your Rights

You may at any time:
1. **Withdraw consent**: Settings → System → Reset Advertising ID (iOS / Android)
2. **Delete data**: Uninstall app to remove all local data
3. **Disable ads tracking**: iOS Settings → Privacy → Tracking → Boxy Pack OFF
4. **Inquire / report**: Email to dugout26.gm@gmail.com — response within 14 days

## 7. Children's Privacy

Boxy is rated **All Ages** on Google Play Store and **4+** on Apple App Store. We do NOT collect personal information from users under 13 (COPPA) / 14 (Korea PIPA).

If we learn we have collected data from a child under that age, we will delete it immediately. Parents may contact us at dugout26.gm@gmail.com to request data deletion.

## 8. Security Measures

- Server-side: AppLovin, Firebase, AppsFlyer all SOC 2 / ISO 27001 certified
